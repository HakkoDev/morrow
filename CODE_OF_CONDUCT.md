# Code of Conduct

We want Morrow to be a welcoming, practical place to build and maintain open-source software.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and considerate, even when disagreeing about implementation details.
- Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Provide useful reproduction steps, context, and constructive feedback.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and maintainers' time. Participation is voluntary.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, while taking the effect of behavior seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing others' private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated bad-faith interactions or attempts to evade moderation are also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the project. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using a private contact method they have published. Avoid exposing private information in public issues. If your concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer where possible.

Maintainers may remove content, request changes in behavior, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and context. They will seek to protect reporters' privacy, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

## Attribution

This document is an original, concise community conduct policy. It may be revised as the project grows.
